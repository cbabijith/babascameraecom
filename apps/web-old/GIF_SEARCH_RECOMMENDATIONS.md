# GIF/Illustration Search Recommendations for Bank Transfer Discount

## What to Search For:

### 1. **Discount/Money Saving GIFs**
- Search terms:
  - "discount gif"
  - "save money gif"
  - "money saving animation"
  - "discount badge gif"
  - "percentage discount gif"
  - "2 percent discount gif"
  - "bank transfer discount"
  - "cashback gif"

### 2. **Bank/Finance Related GIFs**
- Search terms:
  - "bank transfer gif"
  - "neft transfer gif"
  - "money transfer discount"
  - "payment discount gif"
  - "finance discount animation"
  - "banking savings gif"

### 3. **Recommended GIF Characteristics:**
- **Size**: Small/compact (64x64px to 128x128px works well, but larger can be scaled)
- **Theme**: Green color scheme (for savings/discount)
- **Style**: Clean, professional, not too flashy
- **Format**: Animated GIF (`.gif` extension)
- **Content**: Shows discount, percentage, money, or savings concept

### 4. **Popular Sources:**
- **Giphy** (giphy.com) - Search for "discount", "save money", "2 percent"
- **Tenor** (tenor.com) - Search for "bank discount", "money saving"
- **Pinterest** - Search "discount gif animation"
- **Flaticon** - Animated icons section
- **LottieFiles** - Convert Lottie animations to GIF

### 5. **Specific Ideas:**
1. **Discount Badge**: A badge with percentage off (e.g., "2% OFF")
2. **Money Falling**: Money/coins falling into a wallet
3. **Piggy Bank**: Piggy bank filling up or shaking
4. **Calculator**: Calculator showing discount calculation
5. **Checkmark with Money**: Green checkmark with money symbol
6. **Arrow Down + Percentage**: Downward arrow with discount percentage
7. **Gift Box**: Gift box opening with discount tag
8. **Price Tag**: Price tag showing before/after with discount

### 6. **Color Scheme to Look For:**
- **Primary**: Green (for savings/discount)
- **Secondary**: White, light green, emerald
- **Accent**: Gold/yellow (for money)

### 7. **File Requirements:**
- **Format**: `.gif` (animated)
- **Size**: Ideally under 200KB for fast loading
- **Dimensions**: Square format (1:1 ratio) works best
- **Background**: Transparent or light colored

### 8. **Example URLs Format:**
Once you find a GIF, you'll get a URL like:
- `https://i.giphy.com/media/[id]/giphy.gif`
- `https://media.tenor.com/[id].gif`
- Or your own CDN/hosting URL

### 9. **How to Use:**
1. Find your GIF online
2. Copy the direct image URL (right-click → Copy image address)
3. Replace the URL in `checkoutSummary.tsx` at line 67-69
4. Or host it in your `/public` folder and use `/your-gif.gif`

### 10. **Quick Search Suggestions:**
Try these exact searches on Giphy or Tenor:
- "save 2 percent"
- "bank discount"
- "payment discount"
- "money saving badge"
- "discount animation green"

## Recommended: Simple, Clean, Professional
Look for something that:
- Clearly communicates "discount" or "savings"
- Is visually clean and professional
- Uses green color scheme
- Is compact and loads quickly
- Loops smoothly (not jarring)
